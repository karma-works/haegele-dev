This design sketch showcases a dark-themed, futuristic portfolio that blends a "Senior Developer" persona with musical and athletic elements. The layout uses high-contrast neon accents against a charcoal background.

Here are the key regions of the page and their contents:

---

### 1. Navigation Bar (Header)

Located at the top, this sleek, pill-shaped container houses the site's primary links.

* **Logo/Identity:** On the far left, featuring a diamond-shaped icon and the text "SENIOR DEV."
* **Navigation Links:** Centralized links including "HOME" (highlighted with a neon underline), "WORK," and "HOBBIES." 

### 2. The Terminal Hero (Central Focus)

The centerpiece of the design is a floating terminal window that acts as the primary introduction.

* **Window Controls:** Standard "traffic light" icons (red, yellow, green) in the top-left corner.
* **Console Text:** A terminal-style greeting:
* `> Hello, I'm [Your Name].`
* `> Senior Developer.`
* `> Passionate Creator.`


* **Visual Element:** Behind the terminal is a dynamic, teal **heartbeat/waveform line** that stretches across the screen, symbolizing both coding activity and a runner's pulse. see #puplse-background.md

### 3. Personal Catchphrase

Directly below the terminal window is a handwritten-style script in a glowing pink/magenta hue.

* **Content:** "Coding the world, one language at a time." It serves as the transition between the terminal and the lower metric boxes.

### 4. Metrics & Skills Dashboard (Grid Section)

Two symmetrical, glowing cards provide a data-driven look at the creator's life.

* **Performance Metrics (Left Box):** Encased in a pink neon glow, titled "STRAVA STATS." It features a "STRAVX" map visualization, representing running or cycling routes. see #strava-integrations.md
* **Language Levels (Right Box):** Encased in a teal neon glow. It lists technical and spoken languages (Java, Typescript , Deutsch, Español) with corresponding percentage bars and stylized progress indicators.

### 5. Interactive Piano Footer

The base of the design transitions into a musical interface.

* **Keyboard:** A stylized piano keyboard where certain "keys" are highlighted with teal neon outlines.
* **Icons:** Small musical notations (clefs and notes) appear within the keys.
* **Audio Visualizer:** A central concentric circle icon with a speaker symbol suggests that this section is interactive and capable of producing sound.
